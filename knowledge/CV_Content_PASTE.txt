Project: X (Twitter) Account Daily Digest Automation

Date: June 2026 | Geneva, Switzerland
Link: https://www.vinaegg.dev/projects

Vina built an end-to-end n8n automation that monitors a list of X (Twitter) accounts and delivers an AI-summarized daily digest to a Gmail inbox on a schedule, with no manual work.

The pipeline splits the list of account handles with JavaScript, chains two API calls to find each account and fetch its recent posts, uses Claude to summarize them, and combines everything into one digest email sent through Gmail.


Project: OKTA, AI Executive Assistant

Date: June 2026 to present | Geneva, Switzerland
Link: https://www.vinaegg.dev/projects (includes a 1-minute demo video)

Vina built OKTA, her personal AI executive assistant, with Claude Code. It is designed to grow with her over time. She named it after her middle name, Oktaviana: she was born in October, "okta" means eight, and eight on its side is the infinity symbol, for an assistant built to keep growing.
- It works from structured context files (priorities, goals, decision log) and keeps a persistent memory between sessions.
- It acts as the hub for all her projects: each project keeps its own folder, while the assistant tracks status, decisions and where everything lives.
- It is connected to Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack and Canva through MCP, and to ClickUp through a custom API script.
- It chooses the model based on the task: quick research is handed to a cheaper Claude Haiku sub-agent that searches with Perplexity, while deeper analysis is done by a more powerful model.
- When a task repeats, she turns it into a reusable "skill", so recurring requests become automated workflows.

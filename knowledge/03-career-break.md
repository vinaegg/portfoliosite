# Career Break — Family Relocation

From January 2014 to September 2025, Vina took a career break to relocate internationally and take primary responsibility for raising her two children, over 11 years.

Alongside caregiving, she built full local integration in Geneva: she reached B2 French proficiency (DELF B2 certified) and became an active, embedded member of the Geneva community.

She returned to the workforce via the Bring Women Back to Work (BWBW) career re-entry program, turning the break into a deliberate, structured re-entry rather than an unexplained gap. See the Knowledge Article on the BWBW program for what she did during re-entry.

# Career Re-Entry Program (Bring Women Back to Work)

From September 2025 to August 2026, Vina is enrolled in Bring Women Back to Work (BWBW), a 1-year structured career re-entry program in Geneva, Switzerland, for people returning to the workforce after an extended career break.

During the program she earned 5 professional certifications:
- Salesforce Administrator
- Salesforce App Builder
- Salesforce Agentforce
- Workday Basic Series
- Workday Pro Platform Administrator

She also completed a 6-hour Project Management course delivered by Merkle, alongside structured training in workflow design and applied learning.

She converted these certifications into proof of work by building 3 applied technical projects:
1. A Salesforce Experience Cloud site with an Agentforce agent (this site)
2. An AI-directed personal portfolio site
3. An end-to-end n8n automation

See the separate Knowledge Articles on each project for details.
